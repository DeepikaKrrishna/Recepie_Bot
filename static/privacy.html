Privacy notice

Draft. Review it with the project owner (and legal advice if needed) before a public launch, and update it when accounts are added.

What we process

  • What you type (ingredients, filters, dish ideas, questions) is sent to our server and passed on to an AI model provider (OpenRouter and the model it routes to) to generate a response.
  • Technical data: your IP address is held in memory for up to one minute for rate limiting. Server logs record the time, path, status code and response time of each request. Logs do not record what you typed.
  • On your device: your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, advertising trackers or analytics in this version.
  • We don't sell your data.

Third parties

Requests are processed by OpenRouter and the selected AI model provider under their own terms and privacy policies. Some free models may use prompts to improve their services. Don't enter personal, medical or confidential information.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the project maintainers through the project's GitHub repository.
